New package: Angular bindings. The `veiloReveal` and `veiloSkeleton` directives and the standalone `veilo-reveal` and `veilo-skeleton` components, running outside the zone, with zone.js or zoneless, and creating nothing on the server. Built with Angular 20 in partial-compilation mode and supports Angular 20, 21 and 22.
