New package: seasonal loading animations drawn with text characters. The first set is ten effects for Christmas and New Year (snowflakes that grow, frost and aurora colors, snow that settles on skeleton bars, a blizzard that dissolves into content, and flakes that assemble from scrambled characters into cells and dots), each with four flake forms. Use `mountEffect` from plain JavaScript, `SeasonalEffect` from `@veilo-ui/seasonal/react`, or `<veilo-seasonal-effect>` from `@veilo-ui/seasonal/element`. The effects follow the page's Veilo color properties and its theme, pause while off screen, and stay on one frame under reduced motion.
